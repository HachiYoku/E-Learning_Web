# Arun Thai launch checklist

- Confirm the production Arun Thai brand, domain, MongoDB/Cloudinary/Resend/Render configuration, and that no test/UAT database or legacy customer-facing branding is used.
- Confirm the Terms, Privacy Policy, Cookie & Storage disclosure, signup age confirmation, and checkout finality notice in Burmese and English on mobile and desktop.
- Handle privacy requests at `arunthaiedu@gmail.com` in Burmese or English; reasonably verify identity before disclosing/changing data.
- Handle testimonial withdrawal promptly for Arun Thai-controlled channels; record any third-party repost limitation.
- Handle payment problems/disputes using the published payment-error process; verify amounts before refunding.
- Escalate suspected security/privacy incidents to the owner, preserve relevant evidence, and assess containment/communications.
- Owner/team: confirm Myanmar online-sales/eComReg obligations. Obtain external legal confirmation about Thai-representative applicability if intentionally serving users in Thailand.

## UAT

- Adult signup; 13–17 signup with guardian attestation; rejection when age group/attestation is absent.
- Burmese/English Terms, Privacy, Cookie & Storage pages and footer links on mobile/desktop.
- THB and MMK checkout, with and without a valid promo; verify course, currency, final amount, instructions and finality notice before transfer.
- Marketing opt-in remains optional; unsubscribe/re-subscribe behavior remains correct.
- Approved payment shows access/notification; account-deletion warning remains visible.
- Testimonial remains private by default and public identity/avatar follows selected consent.
